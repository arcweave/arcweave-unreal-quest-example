| Dependency | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| [Arcweave Unreal plugin](https://github.com/arcweave/arcweave-unreal-plugin), included as `Plugins/Arcweave` | [Plugin MIT license](Plugins/Arcweave/LICENSE). Its [dependency notices](Plugins/Arcweave/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) cover the bundled Arcscript interpreter and ANTLR runtime. |

The sample has no external model or texture pack. Its station is assembled in C++ from Unreal Engine primitives.
